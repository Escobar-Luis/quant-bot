# UI judging: Day Trader AI variations

Judge: independent subagent, no part in building the variants. Date: 2026-10-17. Evidence: screenshots at 390×844 and 1440×900.

## Scorecard

| Variant | Cool | Useful | Concise | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| trial | 5 | 7 | 8 | 20 |
| stamp | 9 | 9 | 8 | 26 |
| receipt | 8 | 7 | 7 | 22 |
| race | 8 | 7 | 6 | 21 |

## Winner: stamp

1. On the desktop verdict, one ticket card shows the whole story: the "NO" stamp, then "+47% BEATS HOLDING", then "PRISM score 58 → 88".
2. Only stamp's desktop proof fits all three PRISM proofs on one screen: Knowledge Base (6 cited rules), Root Cause and Evaluator.
3. That same proof screen also shows "What we don't know" and "New data check", which scores directly on trust.
4. The learn screen's colour bands go from red to green, with "REJECTED" on Fix 3 and "Beats just holding from here" before Fix 4. A non-trader gets it without help.
5. It is the most memorable: judges will remember the rubber "NO" stamp an hour later.

## Runner-up: receipt

1. "YOU MISSED $5,900" is the plainest, most quotable line in any variant.
2. The learn receipt, "How the AI got to $14,700", adds up line by line, and "VOID" marks the rejected Fridays fix.
3. On desktop, the two receipts side by side show the failure next to the fix and "PRISM SCORE 58 → 88".

## Per variant

### trial
1. Best: The calmest and most Apple-like. "No.", "$10k became $8,200", the better version and PRISM 58 → 88 all fit on one screen at both widths.
2. Worst: Forgettable. The error-bar chart (whiskers, 0% axis, Worse/Better) is a stats graphic that a non-trader has to decode. About 60% of the desktop verdict is empty.
3. Defects: Improvements are coloured red: "Better version +38%" on verdict-lpp and "Fix 2 +21%" on learn. Knowledge Base sits below the fold in the proof, under the chart, Root Cause and Evaluator. On phone learn, Fix 4 sits on the fold.

### stamp
1. Best: Desktop verdict and desktop proof, as cited above. The phone verdict still shows "NO", then the green "+47% / PRISM score 58 → 88" card.
2. Worst: It is the loudest of the four, and the proof is dense. Courses switch only with arrows and dots, with no named course tabs on either width.
3. Defects: The green Better version card is cut off at the bottom of the phone verdict. The phone proof puts New data check and "What we don't know" before the three PRISM proofs, so the proofs fall below the fold. Small labels on the colour bands are hard to read ("found by Root Cause" pill, "PRISM 66" on orange). The proof button is labelled "Why?", not "See the proof".

### receipt
1. Best: "YOU MISSED $5,900" and the learn receipt that adds up to $14,700. The receipt metaphor is distinctive but still calm.
2. Worst: The phone verdict shows only the failure. The better version and PRISM 58 → 88 are hidden behind a "Better version →" swipe, so the phone breaks the "failure, fix, score" beat.
3. Defects: The sign-in screen shows an empty receipt with a lone cursor, which looks broken. In the proof, Evaluator comes after Knowledge Base and below the fold. Small grey monospace text ("01.2023 – 06.2026", "AI PROPOSES · CODE DECIDES") has weak contrast.

### race
1. Best: "Finished last of 3." with 3rd/2nd/1st badges is understood instantly. The bottom nav has a "Proof" tab, and the desktop sidebar shows No./Barely./No. for all three courses.
2. Worst: The busiest screens: lane ropes, swimmers and badges compete for attention. "Heats" and "Pick who swims" are metaphor jargon. "PRISM score 58 → 88" is tiny white text inside a lane.
3. Defects: A light vertical strip runs down the left edge of sign-in at both widths. Small labels inside the lanes are hard to read at 390, and the Fix 4 label crowds the rope. The proof puts Evaluator (58 → 88) last, far below the fold.

## Fix before the demo (winner only)

1. Phone verdict: shrink the stamp about 15% so the green card fits in full above the fold.
2. Rename the "Why?" button to "See the proof" on every course card.
3. Phone proof: put Knowledge Base, Root Cause and Evaluator (58 → 88) straight after the chart, before New data check.
4. Add named course tabs (ZipTrader, Learn Plan Profit, Crypto Wizard) next to the arrows and dots, on verdict and learn.
5. Make the small band labels bigger and higher-contrast, and lighten the grain on the "NO" stamp. Both should pass WCAG AA (the accessibility standard).
