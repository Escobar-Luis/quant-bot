# US evidence for the north-star deck: retail day trading and paid trading courses (research, 2026-10-17)

Tags: **peer-reviewed / regulator / survey / press**. **[R]** = primary text read today; **[A]** = abstract, press or secondary only. "Allegation" = FTC/SEC claim in a settled case, not a court finding. No figure is invented; gaps say "not found".

## Slide candidates
1. US retail options traders lost about $2.1B in 20 months (Nov 2019 to Jun 2021); trading costs alone were $6.4B. JoF 2023 (sec 1.1).
2. FTC, May 2026: one "trading education" MLM took more than $1.2B from consumers since 2018; $795.8M judgment (sec 2.1).
3. FINRA Foundation 2024: 26% of US investors, 61% of under-35s, act on social-media personalities' tips (sec 2.6).

## 1. US retail trader outcomes
1. $2.1B retail options loss (10-day-hold estimate, proxy for retail flow, Nov 2019-Jun 2021); $6.4B aggregate trading costs; 12.6% average spread on weekly options. 2023. **peer-reviewed** [R] Bryzgalova, Pavlova, Sikorskaya, J. of Finance 78(6). https://lbsresearch.london.edu/id/eprint/2827
2. Retail lost about $3B on options around earnings, 5-9% per position (10-14% high-volatility); Nasdaq/PHLX data, Jan 2010-Feb 2021. 2026 (online Oct 2025). **peer-reviewed** [R] de Silva, Smith, So, Review of Finance. https://doi.org/10.1093/rof/rfaf052
3. 36,538 US retail futures accounts (2021-22): median trader lost $100-$200, 60th percentile only breaks even, losses exceed gains. 2024. **regulator** (CFTC staff paper, not peer-reviewed) [R] https://www.cftc.gov/About/EconomicAnalysis/ResearchPapers/index.htm
4. 324 US day traders at one firm (1998-99): 64.2% lost money net of commissions, 35.8% profited; average net about -$750. 2003. **peer-reviewed** [A, abstract] Jordan, Diltz, FAJ. https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/ufajxx/v59y2003i6p85-94.html
5. 66,465 US households (1991-96): most active traders earned 11.4% a year vs 17.9% for the market. 2000. **peer-reviewed** [A, abstract] Barber, Odean, J. of Finance. https://ideas.repec.org/a/bla/jfinan/v55y2000i2p773-806.html
6. Zero-day options: retail lost about $358,000 per day after May 2022 (S&P 500 options). 2023. **press** [A] Bloomberg on a Muenster working paper (SSRN 4404704, blocked, not opened). https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2023-04-21/day-traders-lose-358-000-per-day-gambling-on-zero-day-options
7. Counter-evidence: "relatively small losses" on about $20B of retail trades, 2020-22. 2024. working paper, not peer-reviewed [A] Bogousslavsky, Muravyev. https://experts.illinois.edu/en/publications/an-anatomy-of-retail-option-trading/
8. Scale: about 1.3M US customers were pattern day traders at ten firms (2.4% of margin customers, Jan 2025). FINRA replaced the $25,000 PDT minimum with an intraday-margin rule, effective 2026-06-04 (firms may phase in to Oct 2027). 2026. **regulator** [R] https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/26-10 and https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2025-12/SR-FINRA-2025-017.pdf
9. Non-US, already in the deck: Brazil, 97% of persistent day traders lost money (0.4% beat a bank teller's pay; working paper) https://ideas.repec.org/p/fgv/eesptd/525.html ; Taiwan, 97% of day traders likely to lose money, 74% of volume from past losers (peer-reviewed, RAPS 2020) https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rasset/v10y2020i1p61-93..html

## 2. Paid courses, gurus and finfluencers
1. IM Mastery Academy (iMarketsLive/IYOVIA): more than $1.2B taken from consumers since 2018; $795.8M judgment (most suspended), about $90M assets surrendered. FTC says "very few" members earn substantial money, "many, if not most" lose. 2025-2026. **regulator** [R] https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/05/ftc-state-nevada-take-action-against-im-mastery-academy-deceiving-consumers and https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2026/05/lead-defendants-im-mastery-academy-mlm-scheme-turn-over-tens-millions-dollars-assets-settle-ftc
2. Warrior Trading (day-trading course): $3M settlement; FTC: "the vast majority of customer accounts actually lost money"; refunds of $2.9M+ to 20,402 people. 2022-2023. **regulator** [R] https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/04/federal-trade-commission-cracks-down-warrior-trading-misleading-consumers-false-investment-promises
3. Online Trading Academy: $362M judgment, partly suspended; FTC says most purchasers "made little to no money"; refunds to 31,000+ consumers (count from the FTC's 2021 release title). 2020-2021. **regulator** [R, 2020] https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2020/09/ftc-settlement-requires-online-trading-academy-forgive-consumer-debt-principals-turn-over-millions
4. SEC: 8 social-media "traders" with hundreds of thousands of Twitter/Discord followers, about $100M fraudulent profit (stock manipulation, not a course). 2022. **regulator** [R] https://www.sec.gov/news/press-release/2022-221
5. CFTC or SEC trading-course case 2023-2026: not found (CFTC cases found are pre-2020).
6. 26% of US investors act on a social-media personality's recommendations (61% of ages 18-34); 29% use social media or message boards (n=2,861, survey year 2024). 2025. **survey** [R] FINRA Foundation NFCS. https://www.finra.org/media-center/newsreleases/2025/new-finra-foundation-research-examines-shifting-investor-behaviors
7. Among investors targeted by fraud, 68-69% of finfluencer followers/social-media users lost money vs 26-29% of non-users (small base: only 6% said they were targeted). 2026. **survey** [R] FINRA Foundation brief. https://finrafoundation.org/sites/finrafoundation/files/2026-03/FINRA_Foundation_Research_Brief_Social_Media_Finfluencers.pdf
8. Risky products: 43% of under-35 investors trade options vs 10% of ages 55+; margin 22% vs 4%. 2025. **survey** [R] same FINRA release as 6.
9. Warnings with no statistics and no mention of courses: NASAA finfluencer advisory (Oct 2026) https://www.nasaa.org/82638/informed-investor-advisory-financial-advice-social-media-rise-finfluencer/ ; FINRA "Following the Crowd" (Oct 2026) https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/following-crowd-investing-and-social-media **regulator** [R]
10. Share of US adults who bought a trading course: not found. CFA Institute 2024 finfluencer report exists but its numbers were not read.
