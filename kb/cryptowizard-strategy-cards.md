## When not to trade (19 rules)

### CRYPTOWIZARD-AVOID-1
Rule: Ignore shooting stars that do not form at a WZRD line.
Quote: "If a shooting star is not from a WZRD line"
Source: dc:855852754392449065/859102357852913664 (#wzrd-lessons 2021-06-28)

### CRYPTOWIZARD-AVOID-2
Rule: Avoid trading formations that sit in a poor location or follow pullbacks of previous structures.
Quote: "two examples of formations to avoid trading due to location"
Source: dc:855852754392449065/1194018230369521754 (#wzrd-lessons 2024-01-08)

### CRYPTOWIZARD-AVOID-3
Rule: Prefer under-the-radar assets with solid fundamentals; avoid assets suddenly being talked about by everyone.
Quote: "A secret known by all is not a secret but a trap"
Source: dc:855852754392449065/1216133995654418554 (#wzrd-lessons 2024-03-09)

### CRYPTOWIZARD-AVOID-4
Rule: Use indicators such as the 55 EMA only to check bias, never for trade execution.
Quote: "Do not use any indicators in trading"
Source: dc:855852754392449065/1252725903025377453 (#wzrd-lessons 2024-06-18)

### CRYPTOWIZARD-AVOID-5
Rule: Avoid trading a smaller pattern that significantly opposes the primary pattern.
Quote: "it’s often wise to avoid trading it altogether"
Source: dc:855852754392449065/1305625837562892410 (#wzrd-lessons 2024-11-11)

### CRYPTOWIZARD-AVOID-6
Rule: Avoid low-liquidity assets when trading a sideways market; prefer high-liquidity markets for range trading.
Quote: "avoid low-liquid assets, as they tend to experience extended moves"
Source: dc:855852754392449065/1318313447905169478 (#wzrd-lessons 2024-12-16)
